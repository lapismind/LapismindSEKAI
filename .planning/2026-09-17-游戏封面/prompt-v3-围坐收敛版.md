TASK
Generate one 16:9 landscape illustration for a party card game: four girls playing a magical
card game at a round table in a bright workshop, at the moment the pink-haired girl
successfully summons an Ancient Dragon from her card.

The single most important requirement: this must look like a PRINTED MANGA COLOUR PAGE or a
frame from a 2010s TV anime — flat, matte, hand-drawn. It must NOT look like glowing, glossy,
AI-rendered fantasy art. The STYLE and LIGHT BUDGET sections below are hard rules, not suggestions.

COMPOSITION — A REAL SEATED CARD GAME, NOT A GROUP POSING AT THE CAMERA
- The camera sits above and just outside one side of a round table, tilted down about thirty
  degrees, looking ACROSS the tabletop into the scene. Like a photograph taken from the empty
  seat at a card table.
- Four players sit AROUND the table. One sits on the near side, closest to the camera, seen
  from BEHIND — her back and shoulders fill the lower foreground, cropped by the bottom edge.
  That cropping is intentional.
- The other three sit on the far side, on a receding arc around the round table (the centre
  one farthest away), their heads at three slightly different heights — never on one
  horizontal line.
- Leave quiet space: at least one fifth of the canvas is plain wall or bare tabletop with
  nothing on it.
- Hard rule: nobody stands or sits in a line facing the camera. If the four of them end up
  side by side facing the lens like a group photo, it is wrong.

THE FOUR CHARACTERS

1. NEAR SIDE, seen from behind — the reference with short dark grey-brown hair in a bob.
   The dragon is lunging toward her, so she is RECOILING AWAY from the table: her chair is
   pushed back and tipped, her body arches AWAY from the table (not toward it), one palm
   raised outward toward the dragon, the other hand still holding her card fan low in front
   of her. Her head twists back over her shoulder, eyes wide, mouth open mid-yelp.
   Her back fills the lower foreground, larger than anyone else in the frame.

2. FAR SIDE, CENTRE — the reference with pink hair in one large side ponytail. Protagonist
   and visual centre.
   She has just slammed the dragon card face-up on the table: her LEFT palm is still pressed
   flat on that card. Her RIGHT hand holds her own card fan at chest height, faces turned
   toward the camera. She does NOT raise an arm into the air. She leans forward over the
   table, eyes shut in two delighted arcs, mouth wide open in a triumphant shout.

3. FAR SIDE, LEFT — the reference with long straight pale silver-blue hair.
   SURPRISE: both hands holding her card fan up in front of her chest, leaning back away
   from the dragon, eyes wide, eyebrows up, mouth a small open "o". Startled, not frightened.

4. FAR SIDE, RIGHT — the reference with dark purple hair gathered into a HIGH PONYTAIL tied
   with a light beige-gold scrunchie on top of her head, tail falling down one side. The
   scrunchie must be clearly visible — do NOT draw the hair loose over both shoulders and
   do NOT draw it as a small bun.
   NO REACTION AT ALL: half-lidded eyes looking down at the table, eyebrows perfectly level,
   mouth one short flat line. Absolutely no smile, no shock. She is checking whether the
   summon was even legal. Her fan is held lazily at chest height, faces toward the camera.

- Faces: follow each reference's eye shape exactly; the four girls must NOT share one face
  template. Keep the youthful proportions of the references — teenage girls, not adult women
  with longer faces.

REFERENCE IMAGES
Four character reference images are attached, one for each of the four characters. Each
reference is AUTHORITATIVE for that character's face, eye colour, hair colour, hairstyle and
clothing. Match each character to her own reference only. Do not blend features between them.
The references are CASUAL, everyday outfits — sweaters, jackets, shirts, trousers and skirts.
Everyone stays in these casual clothes. Do NOT put anyone in a stage costume, an idol outfit,
a school uniform, or anything with straps, harnesses or armour.
The references are neutral model sheets: blank expressionless faces, both arms held straight
out to the sides. Do NOT copy that pose and do NOT copy that blank expression. Use only the
poses and expressions described above. Everyone is seated at a table.
Every visible face must wear its own expression, as described above.

THE ANCIENT DRAGON — SOLID, OPAQUE, DRAWN IN THE SAME FLAT CEL STYLE AS THE GIRLS
- The dragon erupts from the face-up dragon card under the protagonist's palm at the centre
  of the table. Its serpentine body grows straight out of the card's artwork, so the card
  reads as the source. One thin ribbon of green light runs along that connection — this is
  one of only TWO glowing things allowed in the whole image (see LIGHT BUDGET).
- The dragon is a SOLID, OPAQUE Eastern dragon: horned head, whiskers, flowing mane,
  jade-green scales drawn as flat colour blocks with ONE darker shadow tone and clean
  outlines. It stands physically in the room, in front of the shelves and the window,
  partially overlapping them where it coils.
- Do NOT make it translucent, spectral, ghostly, made of light, smoking, misting, or
  shedding particles or motes. Only its eyes may shine faintly.
- Its head arcs up beside the protagonist, mouth open in a triumphant roar — small and
  playful rather than terrifying. The girls are startled, not in danger.
- The dragon must not cover any face and must not cover her left hand on the card.

LIGHT BUDGET (HARD RULE)
Exactly two things in the whole image emit light: (1) the face-up dragon card under her
palm, and (2) the thin ribbon of green light connecting that card to the dragon.
Everything else — the room, the dice, the heart tokens, every other card, the girls, the
shelves — is lit by plain daylight and stays matte. If any other element looks like it is
emitting light, remove that glow.

THE GAME'S SIGNATURE — EVERY HAND'S CARDS FACE THE CAMERA
In this game you can see everyone's cards except your own, so every player holds her fan
turned away from her own eyes. All four players therefore hold a fan of five cards tilted
so the card FACES point toward the camera: the near player holds hers low in her hand in
front of her own back; the three across the table hold theirs up with the backs toward
their own faces. The viewer can read all four hands. Do not hide any hand, and keep every
fan clear of the faces.
The cards are portrait rounded rectangles, slightly taller than wide:
- Card faces: a clean coloured medallion in the upper part with one simple flat pictogram
  inside, and a plain light lower panel. The eight pictograms are: a dragon, a ghost, a
  heart, an owl, a lightning storm cloud, a snowflake, a flame, and a potion bottle.
  Icon only — no lettering, no numbers, no words on any card.
- Card backs: warm amber-brown with a small wizard-hat mark.
On the tabletop: the face-up dragon card under her palm, ONE other face-up card, ONE small
stack of face-down cards, a row of small matte candy-pink heart tokens in front of each
player (the near player has only two; the others five or six), and TWO plain bone-white
dice lying on the table. No floating cards in the air. Nothing else on the table.

HANDS
Draw every visible hand with correct natural proportions: a clearly modelled palm,
separated fingers with correct joints, a visible thumb. No oversized flat splayed palm in
place of a hand, and no shapeless hand.

SETTING — QUIET BRIGHT WORKSHOP (MOSTLY EMPTY)
A cheerful sunlit workshop in the daytime, but QUIET: a plain pale cream-lavender wall,
one window with plain daylight sky, wooden shelves that are mostly EMPTY — at most eight
small objects on all shelves combined (a few potion bottles, one rolled scroll, maybe one
potted plant), with wide gaps between them. No workbench clutter, no hanging plants, no
floating objects, no patterned wallpaper. The background stays lighter, quieter and less
saturated than the characters. Not a dim room, not a night scene, not a lounge.

STYLE — FLAT ANIME CEL, MATTE, HAND-DRAWN
- Reference feel: a scanned manga colour page, or a frame of a 2010s TV anime. Matte inks,
  paper-like flatness.
- Clean line art in one uniform warm-dark tone (not pure black) around every form; flat
  base colours; shadows as HARD-EDGED cel shapes in one darker tone per material. No
  gradients, no airbrushing, no soft ambient shading.
- Hair: solid flat shapes with two or three crisp highlight wedges.
- Skin: flat and light with a single soft shadow tone.
- Palette: slightly muted mid-tones overall; saturated accents only on the jade dragon,
  the amber cards and the pink hair.
- FORBIDDEN: bloom; glow beyond the two permitted spots; volumetric light; god rays; lens
  flare; bokeh; depth-of-field blur; film grain; vignette; glossy or wet highlights; 3D or
  painterly rendering; particle effects; mist; floating embers.
- FORBIDDEN: speed lines, sparkles, star flashes, impact flashes, energy auras, magic
  circles. The drama comes from the poses and the dragon itself, not from effects.

CAMERA AND FRAMING
- 16:9 landscape. Camera above and outside the near side of the table, tilted down about
  thirty degrees.
- The round tabletop fills the middle of the frame and recedes toward the far side. The
  near player's back occupies the lower foreground. The three far-side players and the
  dragon fill the upper half.
- Keep every face and the whole dragon comfortably inside the frame. Only the near player's
  back may be cropped by the bottom edge.
- Do not add any chibi, super-deformed or mini version of anyone.
- Do not add any fifth person, animal or mascot.

IF SPACE RUNS SHORT
Keep, in this order: the seated-around-a-table arrangement with the near player's back to
the camera; the protagonist and the dragon as the clear centre; the four distinct
expressions; the four fanned hands facing the camera. Sacrifice background furniture first.
Never solve crowding by turning the seats into a row facing the camera.

TEXT
No letters, numbers, words, logos, watermarks or captions anywhere in the image — not on
the cards, not in the background, not on the spines of the books. Do not add speech bubbles
or sound-effect lettering.

## 相对 v2 改了什么（对照归档五条诊断 + v2 五个落地问题）

| 诊断 / 落地问题 | v2 的写法（病因） | v3 的改法 |
|---|---|---|
| 光效密度（AI 味主因） | `glowing translucent scales`、`glowing dice`、`radiating speed lines… are welcome` 全面放开 | 新增 **LIGHT BUDGET 硬规则**：全图只许两处发光（龙牌 + 连接龙与牌的细光带）；骰子改普通骨白；速度线/星芒/法阵列入 FORBIDDEN |
| 龙是半透明光体 | `glowing translucent jade-green scales, drifting motes of light` | 龙改为**实心不透明**、与人物同一套赛璐璐画法（平色 + 一档暗部 + 描线），明写禁止 spectral/made-of light |
| 元素总数过多 | 书架"potion bottles and rolled scrolls"、`a few loose cards floating` | 书架全场**最多 8 件**且留大空隙；漂浮牌归零；桌面道具逐一点数；要求至少 1/5 画布是安静的墙或空桌面 |
| 线稿软、渲染糊 | 只说 "clean anime cel shading"，没给参照物与禁令 | 锚定"印刷漫画彩页 / 2010 年代 TV 动画截帧"；阴影必须是硬边色块；bokeh/景深/胶片颗粒/湿高光全禁 |
| 四张脸同一模板、偏成熟 | 只写了"youthful proportions" | 加"follow each reference's eye shape exactly；四张脸不得共用模板"；瑞希改**闭眼大笑**（少一张 AI 睁眼脸，也更好笑） |
| 绘名不惊慌（读成惊讶回眸） | "half-turned away, hands up defensively" 太笼统 | 动作落到具体：椅子被推开、身体**后仰远离**桌面、一手向外挡、牌扇拿低了、头拧回肩上喊出声 |
| 真冬有淡笑 | 只说 "NOT smiling" | 更狠：眉毛水平、嘴一条短平线，加一句人设动作"她在检查这次召唤合不合规" |
| 瑞希没牌扇 | 双手都被姿势占用（拍牌 + 高举手臂） | 姿势重排：左手按牌、**右手持牌扇在胸前**，删掉"手臂高举"（也顺带降了戏剧性） |
| 龙与牌连接弱 | 只有"a stream of glowing green light" | "body grows straight out of the card's artwork"——龙身从牌面里长出来，因果画死 |
